Privacy policy

CapSnap is private feedback software for restaurants, made by Hammurabi Coding Company LLC. This page says what the staff app and the guest feedback page collect, how long it is kept, and who can see it.

Guests

When a guest opens a feedback link, the page shows the dish that was photographed and asks for a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and the page has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party fonts or scripts. Your answer goes only to the restaurant that served you. We never turn it into a public review, and every guest sees the same form whatever the rating.

Please do not put personal details in the comment. The link works once, and expires after 30 days if it is not used.

Restaurant staff

The staff app uses the camera, only while you are taking a photo of a dish. It does not use your location, microphone or contacts. The app needs network access to send photos and to show whether it is online.

The restaurant's administrator creates each staff account. We keep the login name, display name, role, and a salted hash of the password (never the password itself) while the account exists. A signed-in phone holds a revocable session, which an administrator can cancel at any time, for example if a phone is lost.

Dish photos are resized on the phone, and their location and camera metadata are removed before they leave it.

Setting up a menu from a website

An administrator or manager can ask the server to read the restaurant's own website and draft a menu. They must first confirm that they own or manage that website. The server then reads only that site's public pages, and only dish names and courses are kept once the draft is checked and saved. We record the website address, the confirmation, which staff member gave it and when, as the restaurant's proof of consent. These records are kept for as long as the restaurant's account exists, unless an administrator asks for them to be removed.

How long things are kept

  • Dish photos on the server: 30 days after they arrive. Photos that never became part of a record: 1 day.
  • Guest feedback (rating and comment): 12 months, then deleted.
  • Guest links: 30 days, or until the first answer. Their short sessions are deleted once expired.
  • Staff device sessions: deleted 30 days after they expire or are cancelled.
  • Server logs: 30 days, with no links, comments or image data in them.
  • Encrypted backups of the above, once switched on: up to 30 days, so a deleted item can remain in a backup for that long.

Who can see it

Each restaurant sees only its own data. Inside a restaurant, administrators and managers can read the guest answers (rating, comment, dish and time) for their locations in the staff app; other staff roles cannot. The operator of this server can access the stored data to run and repair the service. We do not sell data and do not share it with advertisers or analytics providers.
